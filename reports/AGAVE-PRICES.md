# Agave card pricing

The restaurant-provided POS price list supplies 149 matched bottle prices, stored by stable inventory ID in `app/agave-prices.json`. Original POS names are retained beside the price to make spelling/alias matches reviewable. Front cards show CAD prices, with a single taxes-extra note. No serving volume or whole-bottle sale is inferred.

House-infusion cards and their category are withheld from the public catalogue at the owner's request. Their original records remain preserved. La Pinta is a commercial liqueur and retains its own $12 price.

Three existing bottles have no matching supplied price and show “Ask at the bar”: Bosscal Mezcal (68), Los Siete Misterios ancestral Espadín (79), and Pantalones Organic Blanco (120). The generic historical Los Siete Misterios record (78) maps to Doba-Yej $12, supported by its actual archived bottle photo. The POS Ensamble $20 is a separate expression and is not assigned to ancestral Espadín.

Eight POS items are not represented by a matching current catalogue card: Cazcabel Tequila, Chisme Blanco, Ilegal Blanco Mezcal, Los Siete Misterio Ensamble, Ojo de Tigre Mezcal, Patron XO Cafe, San Cosme Joven and Senorio Joven. Their prices are not silently attached to different bottles. Adding these expressions to the collection is separate content work.

Other aliases reconcile the existing record with the supplied SKU label, including 1800 Blanco/Silver, Vida/Clásico, Don Ramón/Dom Ramon Diamanté, El Tequileño/El Tequilano, and Santo Puro Mezquila/Santa Puro Mezcal. Existing researched display names and classifications are preserved.

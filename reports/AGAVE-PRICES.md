# Agave card pricing

Reviewed 17 October 2026 against the restaurant-provided bar inventory. The SPIRITS sheet contains 31 mezcal rows (41–71) and 132 tequila rows (73–204): names in column B and selling prices in column G. Purchase costs, stock counts and other workbook data are excluded from the website and repository.

The comparison identified 150 existing records and 13 additions. Column G updates 12 existing prices and supplies Bosscal's previously missing $16 price. The additions have their own prices. There are now 165 public bottle records and 163 published prices. Permanent IDs and original POS names remain in `app/agave-prices.json`; inventory names and sheet rows trace updated prices. The minimal reconciliation is in `agave-inventory-reconciliation.json`.

Front-card prices sit at the top right opposite the bottle style, replacing the arrow. The bottom text invites guests to explore the bottle. Currency and taxes are stated once above the grid; accessible card names retain CAD. No serving volume or whole-bottle sale is inferred.

House-infusion cards and their category are withheld from the public catalogue at the owner's request. Their original records remain preserved. La Pinta is a commercial liqueur and retains its own $12 price.

Two existing bottles show “Ask at the bar”: Los Siete Misterios ancestral Espadín (79) and Pantalones Organic Blanco (120). The sheet says only “PANTELONES” without an expression, so its $12 is not assigned to Blanco by assumption. Doba-Yej (78) remains $12. The new generic Ensamble (181), at $20, is separate from both Doba-Yej and ancestral Espadín. Sombra and ancestral Espadín remain in the collection although absent from the sheet; Sombra's earlier POS price is retained.

Added Fandango Joven, Ilegal Joven, Los Siete Misterios Ensamble, Ojo de Tigre Joven, San Cosme Joven, Señorío Joven, 1800 Coconut, Cazcabel Jalapeño Blanco, Chisme Blanco, Mi Campo Blanco, Milagro Silver, Patrón XO Cafe and Rooster Rojo Reposado. All thirteen have approved transparent photos. The owner later supplied the Siete Ensamble label, identifying it as Espadín/Cuishe; its venue-specific batch ABV remains unconfirmed. New research and photo provenance are in `agave-research-d.json`.

Other aliases reconcile the existing record with the supplied SKU label, including 1800 Blanco/Silver, Vida/Clásico, Don Ramón/Dom Ramon Diamanté, El Tequileño/El Tequilano, and Santo Puro Mezquila/Santa Puro Mezcal. Existing researched display names and classifications are preserved.

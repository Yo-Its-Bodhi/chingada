# La Chingada baseline source audit

Reviewed 13 October 2026 against Git baseline `e377a3f` (`Sync restaurant status to server clock`). This is a bounded source audit of the existing implementation. Line references below refer to that baseline and will change during the redesign. Browser observations, production search indexing, booking-provider uptime and real-user performance are separate verification work; this report does not claim Lighthouse or Core Web Vitals scores.

## What the redesign must preserve

- **Food and drinks:** 92 menu records, their prices, descriptions, ingredient details, dietary tags and heat levels. Keep the full food, brunch, margarita, cocktail, beer, wine and non-alcoholic selection; category browsing, search, dietary filters and dish details (`app/page.tsx:10–104`, `:321`, `:427–430`, `:524`).
- **Happy Hour:** every day, 4–7 PM; all twelve food items at $6 each; $10 margaritas, $2 off beer, $6 bar rail, 20% off wine bottles. Wednesday's $6 food menu runs all day; drink terms remain subject to service conditions (`app/page.tsx:115–124`, `:180`, `:184`).
- **Thursday AYCE:** $29.95 per person, fourteen taco choices, three tacos for the first round then one at a time, one-hour limit, one order per guest, no sharing, dine-in conditions (`app/page.tsx:117–118`, `:181`, `:185`).
- **Weekly offers:** Monday $10 margaritas, Tuesday any three tacos for $20, Wednesday Happy Hour food all day, Thursday AYCE, Friday $6 bar rail. Brunch is explicitly **coming soon**, with planned Saturday/Sunday 10 AM–2 PM hours; do not silently present it as launched (`app/page.tsx:107–112`, `:182–188`).
- **Booking:** Rezzo iframe at `https://rezzo.bodhix.io`; groups up to six; walk-in guidance; loading and slow-loading states; phone fallback and accessible close control (`app/page.tsx:303–354`, `:486`, `:492–508`, `:579–590`).
- **Agave Library:** legal-age entry; 151 bottle records plus 27 house infusions; category/search controls; detailed origin, ABV, agave, maturation, NOM, production and tasting records; preserve source links and explicit verification limits (`app/agave-data.ts`, `app/agave-archive.ts`, `app/agave-catalog.ts`, `app/house-infusions.ts`, `app/page.tsx:225–254`, `:358–362`, `:525–534`).
- **Business information:** 1242 Dundas Street West, Toronto, ON M6J 1X5; 416-535-2242; reservations@lachingada.ca; Instagram and delivery links; patio first come, first served. Existing hours: Sun noon–9 PM; Mon–Wed 4–10 PM; Thu–Sat noon–11 PM. Toronto-time status uses the server clock where available (`app/page.tsx:260–267`, `:322`, `:479–489`; `app/layout.tsx:95–127`).
- **The food story:** Mexican corn, cooking, grinding and pressing daily; house hot sauces, infusions, dehydrated garnishes, salts/rims/purées and aguas frescas (`app/page.tsx:437–474`). Put this operational craft at the centre of the brand hierarchy.

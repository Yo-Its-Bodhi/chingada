# Agave photograph audit

Inspected all 63 inherited local bottle photographs on four contact sheets, with full-size inspection for ambiguous labels. All 53 original archive photo mappings showed a different bottle or expression. The pattern suggests filenames were assigned from a shifted source list; filenames are not reliable identity evidence.

Updated only image mappings in `app/agave-archive.ts`: 49 corrected mappings, 4 unsupported mappings removed, and 4 verified existing photographs attached to previously unpictured records. The archive now has 53 verified photo assignments. Ten existing assets remain unused because the exact expression is unsupported or the archive has no matching record.

The Casamigos Blanco correction now uses `casa-maestri-reserva-de-mfm-anejo.jpeg`, whose actual label reads Casamigos Blanco. The old `casamigos-blanco.jpeg` is visibly Reposado and is now assigned to Casamigos Reposado. Original photo files remain untouched; no photographs were generated, downloaded or retouched.

## Verification limits and primary references

Most matches use readable brand and expression labels in the inherited photo itself. Decorative Clase Azul decanters were checked against the official brand's regional product descriptions: clear glass Plata, blue feather-painted ceramic Reposado, cream/gold/black Añejo, and black Durango mezcal with its multicoloured beaded cap. The archive's generic `CLASE AZUL` mezcal record specifies Durango, so that image is assigned to the Durango bottle.

- [Clase Azul Plata](https://www.claseazulasia.com/products/tequila-clase-azul-plata)
- [Clase Azul Reposado](https://www.claseazulasia.com/products/tequila-clase-azul-reposado)
- [Clase Azul Añejo](https://www.claseazulasia.com/products/clase-azul-tequila-anejo)
- [Clase Azul Durango](https://www.claseazulasia.com/products/clase-azul-mezcal-durango)

The Amarás-labelled Espadín bottle is a valid image for the archive's older Amores name. The brand's [official FAQ](https://mezcalamaras.com/pages/preguntas-frecuentes) confirms that Amarás/Amores are the same brand and Verde Amarás/Verde Momento are the same product. The image named `agua-santa.jpeg` has the green Mezcal Verde mark and is assigned to the Verde record; its artist label edition is not identified. The record spelling `CENZIO` is retained, while its inspected image clearly reads Logia Cenizo.

This audit verifies photo identity; it does not certify current stock, historical tasting data, ABV, NOM, agave species, production methods or current packaging. The old archive data remains unchanged. Where exact identity could not be verified, the photograph is omitted.
